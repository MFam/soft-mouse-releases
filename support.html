Soft Mouse

Mouse-wheel scrolling as smooth as a trackpad. Separate scroll directions for mouse and trackpad.

Features

Regular wheel mouse

Soft Mouse

With Soft Mouse on, a regular wheel mouse scrolls with the same fluid motion as a trackpad.

More features

Separate scroll directions

Natural scrolling on the trackpad, reversed on the mouse wheel. The one thing macOS won't let you do.

Smooth scrolling

Turns each wheel notch into a glide. Adjust the distance and smoothness to taste.

Per-mouse profiles

Your work mouse and home mouse each keep their own settings. Connected mice are recognized automatically.

Per-app settings

Native scrolling in the terminal, smooth in the browser. Turn Soft Mouse off or change the distance per app.

Cursor effects

A comet tail, sparkles, confetti, or a clownfish, tabby cat or shiba following your cursor. You'll never lose it again.

Mouse pointer speed

A wider speed range than System Settings, applied to the mouse only. The trackpad keeps its own speed.

Support

Getting started

  1. Open Soft Mouse. It lives in the menu bar and walks you through setup on first launch.
  2. Grant the Accessibility permission in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. macOS requires it to smooth and redirect scroll-wheel events. Soft Mouse uses it for mouse-wheel events only.
  3. Scroll with your mouse. Fine-tune direction, distance, smoothness and pointer speed from the menu bar panel or the settings window.

Soft Mouse doesn't appear in the Accessibility list?

Click the + button under the list and choose Soft Mouse from your Applications folder. Or use the "Show in Finder" button in the app's Permission page and drag the app into the list.
